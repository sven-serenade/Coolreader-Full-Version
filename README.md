## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Supports Multiple Formats** — Read ePub, FB2, TXT, RTF, HTML, CHM, and TCR files seamlessly.
- **Bookmark Functionality** — Easily add bookmarks to your reading for quick access.
- **Hyphenation Dictionary** — Improve your reading experience with enhanced text formatting.
- **Table of Contents** — Navigate your eBooks quickly and efficiently.
- **FB2 Support** — The most complete support for FB2 format, including styles and footnotes.
